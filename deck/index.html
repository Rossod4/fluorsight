What we can and cannot claim

No bench experiments have been run. The chemistry is specified from literature. The demo data is synthetic — we wrote it.

So we have no detection limit to quote, and we are not going to invent one.

Our three lab-confirmed samples came back at 34, 41 and 1,840 ng/L. Nothing in between — so every threshold from 23 to 72 gives an identical result. Our own back-test cannot tell us where to put the line. That is why the pilot is stratified.
