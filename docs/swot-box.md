# SWOT box — poster copy

**Drafted 8 September 2026.** For the A0 poster (SWOT is named in the competition's Required
Content). ~150 words of body copy — budget for it before adding.

Evidence marks match the poster header key: **● sourced or unit-tested · ◐ modelled by us ·
○ not yet evidenced.**

---

## STRENGTHS

- ● **Pre-lab.** Every competing platform screens results already paid for. We decide what gets
  paid for.
- ● **Nothing is hidden.** Every point in the score is attributed to a named driver, and operators
  edit the weights themselves.
- ● **Built, public, tested** — fluorsight.co.uk, 27 unit tests, open to any judge right now.

## WEAKNESSES

- ○ **No bench experiment has been run.** The chemistry is specified from literature.
- ○ **No measured detection limit.** Published cyclodextrin LODs span 38 ng/L to 45 µg/L.
- ◐ **The back-test is n = 3, on synthetic data we wrote.**
- ◐ **One probe reads total hydrophobic-anion binding, not PFAS** — surfactants and humic acids
  respond too.

## OPPORTUNITIES

- ● Defra's 2026 PFAS plan and the Environment Agency's national prioritisation map produce
  exactly the queue of sites that then needs sample-level triage.
- ● ~287 English authorities carry Part 2A duties; DWI guidance tightened in March 2025.
- ◐ No UK laboratory publishes a rate card. Price opacity is the customer's daily problem.

## THREATS

- ● **Verralize** is in alpha with a multiplexed sensor array — the closest direct competitor.
- ● The Environment Agency is giving national site prioritisation away free.
- ◐ Break-even is **£62.50/sample**; falling lab prices compress the case.
- ○ The adsorbent is a single-source US product with no stated UK distribution.

---

## Notes on using it

**Every weakness above already has a matching row in the poster's Next Steps panel.** Say that
out loud if a judge presses: the SWOT is not a confession, it is the input to the plan. No bench
data → the paired validation study. Single-source adsorbent → generic β-cyclodextrin polymers are
commercially available from multiple suppliers and the ACS Cent. Sci. 2022 chemistry is
independent of the DEXSORB brand.

**Do not soften the Weaknesses quadrant.** Most entries fill it with "time constraints" and
"limited resources", which reads as having nothing to say. Four specific, quantified technical
weaknesses is the most distinctive thing on the sheet, and it is what makes the Strengths
believable.

**If space forces a cut**, drop one Opportunity and one Threat before touching Weaknesses.
